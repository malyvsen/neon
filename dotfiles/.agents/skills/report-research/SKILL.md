# Write or expand a research report

The objective of this skill is to report on some research that has been done (most likely by you, the AI agent). The main focus is on writing clarity:

1. If you don't already know this, ask the user what the target audience of the research report is - how familiar they are with the research codebase, how familiar they are with the research topic (this is a different matter!) and what other context they have (e.g. whether they know the field in general). If you have a hunch but aren't sure, ask the to confirm.
2. Write what you need to in a manner that will be clear to the target audience (i.e. do not use any terms or mental shortcuts that the target audience won't be familiar with), but otherwise do write concisely - you can assume _intelligence_ on the reader's part, and a preference for skimming a table, list or figure over reading plain text.

Further writing guidelines:

- If there is a key finding, present it in a naturally exposed place (quite often at the top of the report/section you're writing).
- The reader will quite often wonder about the counterfactual (think: control group). If the experiment was done in a way that makes such reasoning not apply, don't bother to mention it - but if it does, try to present the counterfactual in a way that is easy to compare to the actual result.
- The reader will not be aware of this skill's existence, so do not treat terms or mental models from this skill as known to them.
