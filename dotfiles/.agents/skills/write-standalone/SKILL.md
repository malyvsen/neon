# Write standalone

Make the text understandable to someone who has not seen the conversation, and who is familiar with the high-level context of the repository but not with the purpose or methodology of the particular project.

1. Identify terms that the target audience is likely to be unfamiliar with.
2. Introduce them gently by interleaving explanations throughout the text (rather than listing definitions).
3. Write in full, elegant sentences that spell out the connecting logic, but do keep numbers in digit form, e.g. "3-9" instead of "three to nine".
