---
name: write-standalone
description: Write in a way that will be understandable to someone who is out of context. Use when it's clear that the target audience are not familiar with the topic.
disable-model-invocation: no
---

This skill can be invoked to rewrite text, or to write new text. In either case, the goal is to make the text understandable to someone who has not seen the conversation with you, and who is familiar with the high-level context of the repository but not with the purpose or methodology of the particular project.

1. Identify terms that the target audience is likely to be unfamiliar with.
2. Introduce them gently by interleaving explanations throughout the text (rather than listing definitions).
3. Write in full, elegant sentences that spell out the connecting logic, but do keep numbers in digit form, e.g. "3-9" instead of "three to nine".
